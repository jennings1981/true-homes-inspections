# True Homes Inspections — Design Brainstorm

## Design Brief
Home inspection company serving North Idaho (Sandpoint, Coeur d'Alene, Post Falls).
Blend: Professional service website + Mountain/Alpine lifestyle aesthetic.
Reference images: Construction/service site (dark charcoal + amber/yellow accents) + Alpine lifestyle site (cinematic full-bleed imagery, large display type, warm amber accents on dark/neutral).

## Option A — "Rugged Precision"

**Design Movement:** Industrial Mountain Modernism — the visual language of precision engineering meets Pacific Northwest timber craft.

**Core Principles:**
1. Dark charcoal base with amber/gold accent system (inspired by Timbranding1 reference)
2. Asymmetric split layouts — image panels offset against content panels
3. Heavy typographic hierarchy: oversized display numerals and stats
4. Structural yellow accent lines as dividers and decorative frames (like the yellow border boxes in Timbranding1)

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a / #222222) — conveys authority, seriousness, premium
- Primary accent: Amber gold (#F5A623 / #E8A020) — warmth, Idaho sunshine, trustworthy
- Text: Off-white (#F0EDE8) — softer than pure white, more natural
- Secondary: Slate gray (#3A3A3A) for card backgrounds
- Emotional intent: Serious professionals who know their craft, rooted in the land

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with cinematic North Idaho landscape
- Offset two-column sections (image left, content right OR content left, image right — alternating)
- Stats bar with large numerals (years experience, inspections completed, etc.)
- Service grid with icon blocks and yellow "+" decorators
- Sticky header with phone number prominently displayed

**Signature Elements:**
1. Yellow rectangular frame overlays on images (like Timbranding1)
2. Thin amber horizontal rule lines as section separators
3. Large oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) in faded background

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal yellow underlines on nav items
- Service cards lift with subtle shadow on hover
- CTA buttons use amber fill with dark text

**Animation:**
- Hero text slides up on load (stagger per line)
- Stats count up when scrolled into view
- Section transitions: fade + slight upward translate

**Typography System:**
- Display: Oswald Bold — strong, condensed, structural
- Body: Source Serif 4 — editorial, trustworthy, readable
- Accent labels: Oswald Regular uppercase tracking-widest

## Option B — "Alpine Clarity"

**Design Movement:** Scandinavian Mountain Minimalism — clean white space, editorial typography, nature photography as the hero.

**Core Principles:**
1. Light, airy base with deep forest green and warm amber accents
2. Full-bleed cinematic photography (Lake Pend Oreille, Schweitzer) as primary visual language
3. Large serif display type for emotional resonance (inspired by Timbranding2 Alpine site)
4. Generous whitespace as a design element

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Warm off-white (#F7F4EF) — natural, clean, inviting
- Primary: Deep forest green (#2D4A3E) — Pacific Northwest, trust, nature
- Accent: Warm amber (#D4860A) — Idaho sunshine, warmth, action
- Text: Dark charcoal (#1C1C1C)
- Emotional intent: Trustworthy local expert who loves and understands this land

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed hero with large serif headline overlaid on landscape photo
- Asymmetric split: large image left (60%), content right (40%) for About section
- Quote/testimonial section with full-bleed moody mountain background
- Blog preview with editorial card layout

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin amber left-border on blockquotes and highlighted content
2. Large serif display type at 120px+ for hero
3. Black-and-white photography treatment for secondary images

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth scroll with parallax on hero
- Underline animations on links using amber color
- Cards with subtle border reveal on hover

**Animation:**
- Hero image subtle parallax on scroll
- Text reveals with clip-path animation
- Staggered card entrance animations

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display — editorial, prestigious, mountain lodge feel
- Body: Lato Regular — clean, professional, readable
- Labels: Lato Bold uppercase with letter-spacing

## Option C — "Territorial Authority" ← SELECTED

**Design Movement:** Pacific Northwest Industrial Craft — the visual confidence of a regional authority, combining dark professional service aesthetics with alpine warmth.

**Core Principles:**
1. Dark slate base (#18181B) with warm amber (#E8960C) as the single dominant accent
2. Cinematic full-bleed photography with strong typographic overlay
3. Structured asymmetric grid — content never perfectly centered
4. Bold condensed display type paired with clean sans-serif body

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: Near-black slate (#18181B) — authority, premium, night sky over mountains
- Card/section: Dark charcoal (#27272A) — layered depth
- Primary accent: Warm amber (#E8960C) — action, warmth, Idaho gold
- Text: Warm white (#FAFAF9) — natural, not clinical
- Muted text: (#A1A1AA)
- Emotional intent: The most trusted, most experienced inspector in North Idaho — no-nonsense, expert, local

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Sticky nav with phone number + CTA always visible
- Cinematic hero: full viewport, landscape photo, large left-aligned headline, inline quick-quote form on right
- "We Work For You" section: left text + right image with amber frame overlay
- Partner/certification logos strip
- Split section: large photo left, specializations list right
- Testimonials: dark card carousel
- Blog: editorial grid

**Signature Elements:**
1. Amber rectangular border frames overlaid on images (Timbranding1 style)
2. Thin amber accent line before section headings (small amber square/dash)
3. Oversized section labels in faded amber opacity

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Amber underline sweep on nav hover
- Service cards: amber left-border appears on hover
- CTA buttons: solid amber with dark text, slight scale on hover

**Animation:**
- Hero: text stagger slide-up on load
- Scroll-triggered: fade + translate-y for sections
- Stats: count-up animation when in viewport
- Image hover: subtle scale (1.03) with overflow hidden

**Typography System:**
- Display: Barlow Condensed Bold (700) — strong, regional, no-nonsense
- Body: Barlow Regular (400) — clean, modern, readable
- Accent/Labels: Barlow Semi Condensed Medium uppercase tracking-wider
